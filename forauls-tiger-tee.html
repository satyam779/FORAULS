This experience simulates the cloth on your GPU and needs WebGPU.

  • Use a current Chrome, Edge or Opera (version 113 or newer) on desktop, or Chrome 121+ on Android.
  • Safari 26+ on macOS / iOS supports WebGPU. On older Safari, enable it under Develop › Feature Flags.
  • Firefox 141+ supports WebGPU on Windows; elsewhere enable dom.webgpu.enabled.
  • If it's still unavailable, your GPU may be blocklisted. Check chrome://gpu.
